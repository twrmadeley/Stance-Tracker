    Ka'tah reference

    Tap a ka'tah to mark it spent for that unit; tap it again to make the unit readied again (for character abilities or stratagems that re-ready a unit). “Reset round” clears every unit's selection without saving. “Save turn and ready all” stores what each unit used under Saved turns, then clears the selections. A ★ marks a Favoured Ka'tah of a ticked detachment. Everything is saved in this browser on this device. Use Export data under Backup to save a copy you can keep in iCloud, and Import data to load it on another device.
